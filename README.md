# Hereditary Cancer Ecosystem Map

An interactive map of the hereditary cancer ecosystem, built around BRCA1 and BRCA2 as a
proof of concept for a framework that applies to any germline pathogenic variant.

Organizations are placed across six tiers. Filter by gene or disease, click any
organization for its profile, and double-click it to see everything it connects to.

## Evidence

Every connection on this map carries one of two states, and the two are never merged.

**Cited** means a named document states the connection: a ClinicalTrials.gov registration
field, a PubMed author list, or a page on the organization's own website. The link goes to
that document.

**Proposed** means the connection is inferred rather than stated, for example two
organizations appearing together on a large multi-institution paper.

Neither state means verified. Verified requires a person to open the source and confirm it.

No connection on this map was generated by a language model.

## Structure

`index.html` is self-contained and needs no server. Everything runs in the browser.
